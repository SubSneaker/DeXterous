Display & input

Before you start

  1. A Galaxy phone or tablet with One UI 8 or newer.
  2. USB debugging on: Settings › About phone › Software information › tap Build number 7 times, then Developer options › USB debugging.
  3. Plug the phone in and tap Allow when it asks.

Starting DeX…

Your phone is locked

Unlock your phone to keep using DeX.

Rather not unlock each time? Once your phone is unlocked, turn on Keep DeX usable while the phone is locked in the shortcuts panel.

Shortcuts & commands

Everything you type goes to DeX, except shortcuts, which control DeXterous.
